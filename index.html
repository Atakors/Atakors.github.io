Interactive Guide

Windows App Step-by-Step Tutorial

Learn how to configure dimensions, cut guides, posters, and print with exact millimeter fidelity.

01

Open or Drag & Drop Your Image

Launch PrintMaster A4. Click Browse Image... in the top-left or simply drag and drop any photo directly onto the large blueprint workspace. Supported formats: PNG, JPG, JPEG, BMP.

02

Pick a Standard Size Preset (or Custom mm)

Select your required dimensions from the dropdown. One click sets exact legal standards:

🛂 Passport / ID: 3.5 × 4.5 cm 🇺🇸 US Visa: 5.1 × 5.1 cm 💳 Business Card: 8.5 × 5.5 cm 📸 Polaroid: 7.0 × 7.0 cm ✉️ A6 Postcard: 10.5 × 14.8 cm

💡 Leave "Lock Aspect Ratio" checked to preserve natural photo proportions!
03

Adjust Margins & Auto-Fit the Page

Set paper margins: choose Standard (10 mm), Narrow (5 mm), or Borderless (0 mm). Keep Auto-Fit Duplicates checked, and PrintMaster will automatically calculate how many copies fit your A4 sheet in the optimal pattern (e.g. 15 images in 3 × 5).

04

Turn On Scissors Cut Marks ✂️

Expand Advanced Print & Cut Options and make sure Show & Print Cut Marks is enabled. The app draws precision corner tick marks and a faint guide box around each image, letting you cut straight with a ruler or paper cutter without guessing.

05

Export Multi-Page PDF or Print Directly

Click Print to PDF / Paper to send straight to your printer, or click Export PDF for a crystal-clear vector document. You can also export an uncompressed 300 DPI Image.

01

Switch to Poster Maker Mode

In the sidebar under Operation Mode, click the Poster Maker card. The interactive blueprint preview will immediately switch from a single-page grid to a multi-sheet wall tile view.

02

Choose Your Wall Poster Dimensions

Select how many A4 sheets to split your image across:

2 × 2 Sheets (~A2 Poster, 42 × 60 cm) 3 × 3 Sheets (~A0 Giant, 63 × 89 cm) 4 × 4 Sheets (Massive Wall Mural, 84 × 118 cm) Custom Across × Down Grid

03

Set Overlap (Glue Allowance)

Select an overlap margin between adjacent sheets. The standard is 10 mm (Std). This creates a clean strip where sheets overlap so you never have gaps or white seams between pages.

04

Enable Glue Lines & Sheet Coordinate Labels

Keep Print glue lines & sheet labels checked. Each printed sheet will feature a dashed overlap line saying "Glue Sheet #2 Here" and clear coordinates (Row 1/2, Col 1/2) so assembling your poster is foolproof.

05

Export Multi-Page PDF & Assemble

Click Export PDF. All pages are bundled into one sequential PDF. Print each page on regular A4 paper, trim along the marked cut edges, apply glue or tape along the designated overlap strips, and your giant wall poster is ready!

⚠️ The #1 Fatal Mistake Beginners Make: Wrong Scaling!

When opening a PDF in Adobe Acrobat, Chrome, or Windows Photo Viewer, printer drivers almost always default to "Fit to Printable Area" or "Shrink to Fit". This shrinks your photos by 4% to 8%, ruining official passport regulations!

  • ✅ ALWAYS SELECT: "Actual Size" or "100% Scale" in your printer settings dialog.
  • ✅ Paper Size: Ensure the printer is set to A4 (210 × 297 mm), not US Letter (215.9 × 279.4 mm).
  • ✅ Orientation: Match the orientation selected in PrintMaster (Portrait or Landscape).
01

Recommended Paper Types

• Passport & ID Photos: Use 180–240 g/m² glossy or satin photo paper for sharp, non-bleeding facial details.
• Stickers & School Labels: Use A4 self-adhesive vinyl or glossy sticker paper.
• Wall Posters: 120–160 g/m² matte presentation paper gives rich color depth without reflections.

02

Print Quality & DPI

In your printer properties, choose "High" or "Best Quality" instead of "Standard/Draft". PrintMaster outputs images at ultra-crisp 300 DPI, so your printer's finest droplets will capture every micro-detail.

⚡

Batch Printing (50, 100, 200+ Copies in One PDF)

Need 100 event badges or 50 employee photos? In the Copies & Batch Export panel, type 100 or tap one of the quick preset buttons (20, 50, 100, 500). PrintMaster will automatically divide them across the exact number of A4 sheets needed and export a single, seamless multi-page PDF!

⚡

Auto-Rotate 90° for Maximum Yield

Enable Auto-rotate 90° for max yield under Advanced Options. If rotating your image horizontally or vertically squeezes extra copies onto the page, PrintMaster does the math automatically to save expensive photo paper.

⚡

Custom 4-Way Asymmetric Margins

Click "Custom 4-Way Margins" to enter independent Top, Bottom, Left, and Right margins. This is ideal when printing sheets that will be hole-punched for 2-ring/4-ring school binders or spiral bound.

⚡

Live Blueprint Canvas Zoom Controls

Use your mouse scroll wheel or the View menu (Ctrl +, Ctrl -, Fit to Screen) to inspect millimeter lines and corner cut marks up close before hitting print.
